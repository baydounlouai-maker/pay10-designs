# 05 · People: "The subtitled film"

**Rationale.** Pay10's 15-second brand film already tells the whole story: a deal is signed upstairs, and next door a family pays for a teddy bear with one scan. The homepage treats that film as its script. The hero plays it in a letterboxed screen with live subtitles in Pay10 yellow. Below it, the page pauses the film at four moments, one for each audience (consumers, merchants, enterprises, banks and partners), and each moment carries the products that make it happen. Every still has a timecode that jumps the film back to that scene. Navigation follows the people: a rail of stories, with a full site index one click away. Markets work as a picker with each regulator, licence and local office. The page closes on the leadership team and a contact form that opens a pre-filled email.

**Palette.** Daylight #FFFFFF · Wine ink #2A0B14 · Wine #4A0E1E · Pay red #ED1C24 (red text #C4121B) · Sun #FDB913 · Rose #D9B3BB · Muted #7A5560

**Type.** Parkinsans (300–800), one family.

**Memorable thing.** Yellow cinema subtitles under real frames from Pay10's own film.

Stills in `frames/` are frames taken from `assets/hero.mp4`.
