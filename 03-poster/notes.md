# 03 Poster: "Three Sheets"

**Rationale.** Pay10's line "One platform. Every payment. Anywhere" has three beats, and the logo has three colours, so the page is built on both. The cover prints each beat on its own flat yellow, orange or red field. Each field links to a matching sheet: yellow for what you can use (the POS10, P5 and P10 cut-outs standing on their names, then products and solutions), orange for how you get paid and the live MDR calculator, red for where Pay10 is licensed (five markets drawn as a transit line in the order Pay10 entered them, with certifications and clients pasted on as white labels). News, company facts, offices and the regulatory footer follow. A tricolour header strip fills as you read. There are no gradients, rounded cards or video.

**Palette.** #FDB913 yellow, #F58220 orange, #ED1C24 red, #000000 black, #FFFFFF white. Text on yellow and orange is black. White sits on red only at display size.

**Type.** Archivo is the only typeface, used across its width axis: 125% for display, 75% for the cover on phones, 100% for text.

**Memorable thing.** The cover, where the headline also works as the colour-coded contents page.
