# Rails: Pay10 Global homepage

**Rationale.** Pay10 builds the rails money travels on between regulated markets, so the homepage is a transit network. The five licensed markets are stations on an ink railway line that represents the licensed network. Products are coloured lines that stop only where content.json lists them. The page is one line that leaves the logo's "10" roundel, stops at each section and ends at a terminus roundel. The structure works like a concourse. A route planner ("I am a business, starting from Dubai") takes each visitor to their regulated entity, licences, history, local products, solutions or leadership, and local contact. After it come a product line chart, a savings calculator, proof, news and offices.

**Palette.** Ink #1A2233, paper #FFFFFF, platform #F1F3F5, rule #D5DAE1, signal red #ED1C24 (Pay10 World, "you are here"), signal yellow #FDB913 (signs, HQ, results board). Lines: #D1366F, #EC7A1C, #0F8A7F, #2D6BC4, #6A45B8, #3A8E3E.

**Type.** Barlow Semi Condensed (signage, headings, plates), Barlow (body).

**Memorable thing.** The network map lights up your lines as you plan your route.
