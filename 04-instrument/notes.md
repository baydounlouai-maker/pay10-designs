# 04 · Instrument

**Rationale.** The page is a precision tool, not a brochure. It opens on a working instrument: three engraved scales (volume, share, MDR) with a live readout set as an accounting statement. Fees appear in red, the Pay10 fee in parentheses, and the yearly total has a double rule under it. One market setting (header select, instrument dial or the network strip) recalibrates the whole page: which jobs are live there, the licence certificate with its regulator, entity, history and verbatim disclosure, the local office, and the default currency. Products are grouped by the job a merchant needs done, not by product name. Every figure comes from content.json.

**Palette.** Ledger paper #ECEEEA, plate #F8F9F6, slate ink #1C2B3F, secondary #4D5A68, rule #C3C9CC, Pay10 red #ED1C24 (text #B8141A), roundel yellow #FDB913.

**Type.** Archivo (variable width + weight) used throughout: expanded for display, normal for reading, condensed for scale legends. Figures are tabular everywhere.

**The memorable thing.** On the MDR scale, Pay10's ~0.65% marker sits next to the 1–3% band, and the yellow span between it and your rate is the money you keep. The readout then stays docked at the bottom of the screen as you read on.
