# Khatam (02 · Institution)

**Rationale.** The eight-point star used in Gulf lattice work is called *khatam*, which is Arabic for "seal". A central-bank licence is also a seal, so one shape does two jobs here. In the hero it is a carved screen that opens onto Pay10's own film. In the register it is a red seal against each licence. The page is laid out in the order a regulated group's visitors need: who you are and who licenses you, then a register of jurisdictions (regulator, legal entity, licences, which products are live in each market), the record from 2017 to 2026, the payment flow, the savings calculator, governance and assurance, announcements, and the six offices.

**Palette.** Oxblood #3A0C14 (the maroon of the film's interiors), raised #4A1420, footer #2A0910, gypsum #F6F1EF, ink #2B1216, ink-soft #6B5156, rose #E3C6CA, Pay10 yellow #FDB913 (used as brass), Pay10 red #ED1C24 (seals only), and red text #C4141B.

**Type.** Gilda Display for display text. Readex Pro (variable) for text: it is geometric, close kin to Outfit, designed with Arabic.

**Memorable thing.** The mashrabiya opens. A closed lattice of oxblood bars with brass inlay thins out to reveal the Dubai film behind a pointed arch.
