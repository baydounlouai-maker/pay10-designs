Our journey so far

  1. 2017

    Pay10 was founded in India, focusing on secure online payment solutions for businesses.

  2. 2018

    Pay10 launched its first suite of products, giving businesses integrated digital payment solutions.

  3. 2019

    Pay10 worked with high-profile enterprise clients, including Indian Railways and ICICI Lombard.

  4. 2020

    Pay10 went live with 100+ payment options, payouts and automated settlement.

  5. 2021

    Pay10 expanded its proposition and operations, with mPOS and multiple modes of payment.

  6. 2023

    Pay10 opened its new headquarters in Dubai, UAE, to expand globally.

  7. 2024

    Digital wallet solutions focused on domestic payment schemes and local payment methods.

    • Stored Value Facility (SVF) and Retail Payment Services & Card Schemes (RPSCS) licences from the Central Bank of the UAE
    • Payment Aggregator Cross Border licence from the Reserve Bank of India

  8. 2025

    Expansion of digital payment products into key markets in the Middle East, Africa, Asia and Europe.

    • Open Finance Payment Initiation approval from the Central Bank of the UAE
    • Payment Services Licence from the Central Bank of Bahrain
    • Payment Institution Licence approval from Bank Al-Maghrib

  9. 2026

    Regulated expansion into Bahrain under the Central Bank of Bahrain.

    • Entry into the European Union through Pay10 EU Kft’s Electronic Money Institution licence, granted by the National Bank of Hungary

Regulatory information

PAY TEN PAYMENT SERVICES PROVIDER L.L.C (“Pay10 UAE”) is licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE (“CBUAE”) and holds an Open Finance Licence (APM, POS), Retail Payment Services (RPS – Category II) authorization for Merchant Acquiring Services, Account Issuing, Payment Aggregation Services, Domestic Fund Transfer Services, and Cross-Border Remittances, as well as Stored Value Facilities (SVF) authorization for Opening & Maintaining Digital Wallets and Payment Aggregator – Online (PA-O) services. Services are provided in accordance with applicable UAE laws, regulations, and regulatory requirements.

Pay10 Services Private Limited (“Pay10 India”) is authorized and regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (“RBI”) as a Payment Aggregator – Online (PA-O), Payment Aggregator – Cross Border, Import & Export (PA-CB I&E), and Prepaid Payment Instrument Issuer (APM, POS). Services are provided in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, circulars, and directions issued by the Reserve Bank of India and other competent authorities.

PAY TEN B.S.C CLOSED (“Pay10 Bahrain”) is licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of Bahrain (“CBB”) as a Type 7 Ancillary Business Provider – Payment Services Provider Licence (APM, POS). Services are provided in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and directives issued by the Central Bank of Bahrain.

PAY TEN MAR (“Pay10 Morocco”) is licensed and regulated by Bank Al-Maghrib (“BAM”) as a Payment Institution Licence (APM, POS) holder. Services are provided in accordance with applicable Moroccan laws, regulations, and supervisory requirements.
