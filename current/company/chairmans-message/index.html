Meet Our Board

  • Harry Gill

    Founder and Chairman

    Prabhpreet Singh Gill (Harry Gill) is a visionary fintech leader shaping the future of global payments. Known for his clarity of thought and execution, he founded Pay10 to build secure, interoperable financial infrastructure that allows money to move freely, directly, and without friction across borders. Under his leadership, Pay10 has grown into a globally regulated fintech, working in close partnership with regulators and financial institutions to advance open finance and modern payment ecosystems. Driven by a belief that interoperability is the true backbone of digital finance, Mr. Gill continues to lead Pay10 with a long-term vision focused on trust, inclusion, and sustainable innovation.

  • Saad Kaleem

    Global CEO

    A forward-thinking leader with extensive expertise in fintech, payments, and business transformation, Saad Kaleem is known for his strategic vision and ability to drive impactful results, successfully leading organisations through periods of rapid growth and innovation. Leveraging his extensive experiences, Saad is charting a path for global expansion and is well on his way to positioning Pay10 as a forerunner in the fintech landscape. He is passionate about building strong teams, driving operational excellence, and creating value for client, partners, and stakeholders in an ever-evolving digital economy.
